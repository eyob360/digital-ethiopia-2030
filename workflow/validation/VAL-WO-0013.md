## Confidence behavior change — assessed as strictness increase, not drift

Confidence `85` (or any value outside 0–1) was previously rescaled by /100 and could be auto-inserted; it is now rejected. BRD-0002.R10 thresholds are stated on the 0–1 scale (0.85 / 0.6); BRD-0002.R8 requires rejecting non-strict extraction output; the OpenAI prompts in the workflow request `"confidence": number` with no scale — no BRD, prompt, or recorded decision (D-0001–D-0021 scanned) documents a 1–100 scale. Rejecting undocumented-scale values strengthens R8.AC3/R10 enforcement and was an explicit in-scope decision of the WO. Consequence in the workflow: such outputs route to the fallback path exactly like other invalid JSON (wiring unchanged).
